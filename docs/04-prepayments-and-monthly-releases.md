# Prepayments and monthly releases

## Established model

When an annual service is paid in advance:

1. Record the annual supplier bill to **620 — Prepayments** for the covered service period.
2. Create monthly bills/releases for the months receiving the service.
3. Date each monthly bill according to the supplier's established convention, normally the first of the service month.
4. Post the monthly amount to the historically appropriate expense account.
5. Clear each monthly bill against the prepayment using the established Xero process.
6. Confirm that all months are present exactly once and that the total releases equal the annual prepayment.

Monthly amount for an evenly allocated annual fee:

`annual prepayment ÷ number of covered months`

Check the contract/service period before assuming twelve equal months.

## Secretaries Colombo — invoice 1161

- Annual bill: Rs96,000.
- Service period: 1 June 2024–30 May 2025.
- Annual account: **620 — Prepayments**, tax type NONE.
- Monthly release: 12 bills from June 2024 through May 2025.
- Monthly amount: Rs8,000.
- Monthly expense account: **412 — Administration Expenses**, tax type INPUT.
- Status: all twelve monthly bills already exist and are paid.

Do not create another monthly series for invoice 1161.

The June–August 2024 bills used the first of the month as both bill and due date. From September 2024 onward the monthly bills remained dated on the first and generally used month-end as the due date. Preserve the newer convention unless a source document requires otherwise.

## Secretaries Colombo — invoice 1352

- Annual bill: Rs96,000.
- Service period: 1 June 2025–30 May 2026.
- Annual account: **620 — Prepayments**, tax type NONE.
- Monthly release: Rs8,000 per month to **412 — Administration Expenses**.
- Status: monthly bills exist from June 2025 through May 2026.
- Exception: May 2026 is Authorised with Rs8,000 outstanding and no payment recorded at the 11 September 2026 review.

## Expense-account decision

Xero also contains active account **433 — Secretarial Expenses**. However, the two observed Secretaries Colombo annual cycles use account 412 for their monthly releases. Under the supplier-consistency rule, continue using account 412 unless finance expressly approves a reclassification to 433. A change to 433 should be applied prospectively or through an approved correction plan, not mixed unpredictably within one service cycle.

## B Plus reference pattern

B PLUS (PVT) LTD provides the software-subscription comparison used in training:

- annual payments are held as prepayments;
- monthly bills are created on the first of each month;
- the monthly bills use month-end due dates;
- the monthly expense account is **500 — Software Subscriptions**; and
- monthly draft bills exist for future periods and are processed according to the normal review cycle.

Different B Plus invoice numbers represent separate subscription renewals and monthly amounts. Do not combine them only because the supplier is the same.

## Other prepayments

The PickMe/Digital Mobility example is a Xero prepayment, not an ordinary supplier bill. The training did not establish its covered service period or monthly release schedule. Confirm that evidence before creating releases.
