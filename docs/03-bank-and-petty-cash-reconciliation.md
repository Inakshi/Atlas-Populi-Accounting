# Bank and petty-cash reconciliation

## General matching procedure

1. Confirm the statement account and statement period.
2. Check the source voucher/bill before accepting a suggested match.
3. Compare date, payee, reference and amount.
4. Use Find & Match where one statement line settles multiple active bills.
5. For partial payments, select only the relevant active allocations.
6. Reconcile the statement line.
7. Review remaining unmatched lines and the overall account at the same cutoff date.

Search by amount when the statement reference is autogenerated or differs from the PV reference, but never use amount alone. Repeated supplier amounts can refer to different months or phases.

## Petty cash

The trained process is:

1. Review the voucher and recorded bill.
2. Record the historical petty-cash payment in Xero using the voucher date and reference.
3. Import the petty-cash statement.
4. Match the statement line by date, payee, reference and amount.

When one voucher combines several bills, use Find & Match. The training example for voucher 008 combines Rs4,130 and Rs41,487 into one Rs45,617 statement line.

The training identified Xero petty-cash transactions that predate the available uploaded statement history. Therefore, matching recent lines does not prove the total petty-cash balance. A clean close needs:

- an approved opening-balance/cutover bridge;
- Xero and statement balances at a common cutoff;
- physical cash verification; and
- review/sign-off evidence.

## Supplier payments and batches

- Match each bank debit to active Xero payment allocations.
- One PV can support several separate bank debits, as in the payroll example.
- Equal recurring amounts must be distinguished by period and payment date.
- A deleted full-value payment must not be included with active partial payments.

## Transfers

Create an inter-account transfer once, then match the statement counterpart in both accounts. “Create once” does not mean that one statement side can remain unreconciled.

The demonstrated LKR transfer was Commercial Bank to Petty Cash for Rs200,000 on 14 May, cheque 908124. It is separate from the Rs200,000 PickMe prepayment on 5 May.

## Foreign currency

The demonstrated flow is:

1. Match customer receipts in USD Union to the relevant sales invoices.
2. Record USD Union to LKR Union using the actual USD sent and LKR received.
3. Derive/check the implied exchange rate from the actual amounts.
4. Record and match LKR Union to LKR Commercial.
5. Resolve any bank fee separately.
6. Finish each transfer pair before starting the next.

The customer-receipt allocation step was assigned to Inakshi in the training but was not fully demonstrated. Do not assume how differences should be allocated without the source invoice and receipt evidence.

## Bank fees and direct receipts

- Bank charges use **401 — Bank Fees**. The bank name shown in the reconciliation form is the contact, not a separate bank-specific GL account.
- CNC operational-support receipts use **202 — Income from Operational Support Services**, with the supported description and Department.
- Unexplained cash receipts must remain unresolved until their source and coding are established. The Rs42,000 asset-disposal receipt in training was deliberately left pending.
- VAT refunds are entered as Receive Money to **314 — VAT Input**, using the actual tax period and a backlink in the VAT refund tracker. The sampled aggregate refund has no Department.

## VAT refund example

The FY25/26 VAT tracker records period 2611 (January 2026), refund processed by IRD of Rs401,728 on 30 April 2026. The linked Xero receipt is dated 5 May 2026, reference **VAT Refund 2611**, for Rs401,728 to VAT Input, Tax Exempt, with no Department. It is reconciled.

The IRD processed date and bank receipt date describe different events and should both be preserved. Other tracker rows show that tax liabilities can be offset before receipt, so the refund notice amount may differ from the cash received.
