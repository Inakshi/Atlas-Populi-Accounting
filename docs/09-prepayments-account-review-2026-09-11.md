## Control procedure for account 620

At each month-end:

1. Export or inspect the full 620 ledger through the close date.
2. Group movements by supplier and originating invoice or prepayment object.
3. Tie each originating base amount to a service period and expense account.
4. Compare expected releases through the close month with existing Draft, Authorised and Paid bills.
5. Check that tax and levy lines are excluded from the base release where they were posted separately.
6. Correct duplicate, wrong-month and wrong-amount bills before approval.
7. Apply or pay the approved release from 620 using the supplier's established Xero method.
8. Read the record back and confirm the supplier schedule's cumulative releases and remaining unexpired balance.
9. Keep security deposits, advances and historical clean-up items on a separate exception list.
