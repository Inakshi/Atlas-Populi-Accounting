## Atlas Populi and Atlas Labs

Atlas Labs is the Australian parent company, and Atlas Populi is its wholly owned Sri Lankan subsidiary. Atlas Populi manages the people who carry out the work and deliver services for Atlas Labs. It functions as the group's delivery operation and cost centre, while earning service revenue in its own accounts by invoicing Atlas Labs for that work. The two entities maintain separate financial records.

## Service delivery and revenue

Atlas Populi delivers work through projects and invoices Atlas Labs for the services provided. Project billing uses hourly rates on a time-and-materials basis. For example, software engineers work towards a project's deliverables, and their time forms the basis of the service charge. Atlas Labs pays Atlas Populi in USD, with receipts currently paid into Atlas Populi's Union Bank account.

The parent–subsidiary relationship makes arm's-length pricing an important consideration for these intercompany charges. The detailed pricing method, supporting records and invoicing procedure belong in the relevant SOPs.

## Cost structure

Payroll is Atlas Populi's largest cost, reflecting its role in providing the people who deliver services for Atlas Labs. It includes wages and salaries for delivery and support staff, together with employer contributions. Staffing levels and the mix of people required for project work are therefore central to its cost base.

Project consulting and office rent are also significant costs. The delivery operation is supported by software subscriptions, telephone and internet services, utilities, cleaning, security and office supplies. Project-related expenditure also includes components, tools, packaging and transport. These costs support the workforce and the practical requirements of delivering the work.

## Relationship with Horcery

Horcery is a separate, related company. Atlas Populi performs work for Horcery through Atlas Labs, and Horcery does not pay Atlas Populi directly. Atlas Populi's billing relationship for this work is with Atlas Labs.

When Atlas Populi incurs expenditure on Horcery's behalf, it bills Atlas Labs for reimbursement. Atlas Labs then recovers that expenditure from Horcery. This keeps the reimbursement relationship aligned with the route through which Atlas Populi provides the work.

## Financial year and legal framework

Atlas Populi's financial year ends on 31 March, and its working timezone is Asia/Colombo. These give reporting periods and cutoffs a common reference.

As a private limited company incorporated in Sri Lanka, the company operates under the Companies Act No. 7 of 2007, as amended. It must maintain proper accounting records, prepare financial statements and meet statutory filing requirements with the Registrar of Companies. Separately, it must meet corporate income tax filing and payment obligations under Sri Lanka's Inland Revenue Act. Additional obligations, including VAT and payroll tax deductions, apply where the relevant conditions are met.
